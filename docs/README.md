## Frozen plans and historical checkpoints

[Main-study protocol](main_study_protocol_v1.0.md) and
[statistical analysis plan](statistical_analysis_plan_v1.0.md) preserve the
prospective design and their original checkpoint language. Unchecked boxes and
phrases such as "no Test response has been collected" describe those dated
checkpoints, not the present state. The SAP remains byte-for-byte frozen.

The later, more specific SAP and [M4 manifest](m4_methods_manifest_v0.1.md)
define the executed statistical and generation settings. For example, the
early protocol's general interval guidance is operationalized by the SAP's
model-level Wilson intervals and paired-difference cluster bootstrap. Historical
timelines, prices, availability checks, and authorization conditions are not
current instructions to rerun the models or evidence of current provider prices.

Candidate, M3, M5, M6, and M7 reports preserve the state at their respective
dates. Open tasks in an older report do not reopen a subsequently completed
gate. Submission-revision notes describe preparation at those revisions; the
current status page records the author's later submission.

Original numerical exports and the v1.0 supplementary workbook are retained
for provenance. Use the amended reporting status, not the old generated H3
`hypothesis_supported` field, when interpreting the study.
