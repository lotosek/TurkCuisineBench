# Controlled access to active Test evidence

Effective 17 September 2026. Public Dev materials and aggregate results follow
`LICENSES.md`; they do not require an access request. Active Test and private
review provenance are not covered by the public licenses.

Send requests to bugrayilmaz@klu.edu.tr, identifying the requester and institution
or independent research role, a verifiable contact, the audit/replication purpose,
required fields, intended users, and how Test exposure and onward disclosure
will be prevented. Institutional employment is not itself a prerequisite.
Journal requests should use the journal's verified editorial channel.

Requests are assessed against scientific purpose, minimum data needed, security
and contamination risk, and third-party or contributor rights. Access is not
automatic. Approved recipients receive a defined subset through an agreed
private channel with written non-redistribution and secure handling conditions
for private material. Reviewer identity keys and blinding secrets are not
supplied. Redacted evidence or aggregate verification may be offered where full
access is unsuitable. No fixed response time or long-term hosting guarantee is
asserted.

The editorial package contains frozen Test evidence, response-level consensus
and independent-rating/adjudication records, excluding identity keys, credentials
and blinding salt. It must not be uploaded as a public journal supplement.
Publicly licensed components retain their licenses wherever supplied.

Any public Test-v1 release will be separately versioned after an explicit release
decision and deployment of a successor active Test. No release date is promised.
