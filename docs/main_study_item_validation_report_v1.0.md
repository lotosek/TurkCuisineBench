# Main-study item validation report v1.0

Status: **M3 complete; private Test content frozen. M4 was open at this report date and was subsequently completed on 2026-08-31.**
Date: 2026-08-28

## Scope and independence

Two independent validators reviewed all 72 candidate items against their official or institutional sources. The prospectively locked 18-item set remains the primary agreement analysis, and the prespecified 30-item risk set remains the sensitivity analysis. Because both validators also completed the other items, an all-72 comparison is reported only as supplementary evidence.

The lead researcher subsequently adjudicated the disagreements. This adjudication is analytically distinct from independent validation and is not counted as a third independent review. Original reviewer labels, rationales, and pre-adjudication decisions are preserved in the private audit record.

## Pre-adjudication agreement

| Analysis set | N | Agreements | Disagreements | Raw final-action agreement | Cohen's κ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Prospectively locked primary set | 18 | 17 | 1 | 94.4% | 0.000 |
| Prespecified risk sensitivity set | 30 | 26 | 4 | 86.7% | 0.000 |
| Full set, supplementary | 72 | 61 | 11 | 84.7% | 0.000 |

Reviewer B assigned `accept` to every item, creating a one-category marginal distribution. The resulting κ values are therefore not interpreted as evidence of poor reliability in isolation; raw agreement, marginal distributions, the risk-focused sensitivity result, and the adjudication record must be reported together.

## Adjudication outcome

- Eleven disagreements were routed to lead-researcher adjudication.
- Ten final decisions adopted Reviewer A's source-grounded revision.
- One final decision used a custom source-grounded revision.
- Sixty-one items remained unchanged.
- Eleven items were revised before model execution.
- No item was excluded.

Every final item has two completed independent review records, a retained official-source URL, a fixed question and accepted-answer inventory, and an explicit final status. Design-stage ambiguity flags were not retrospectively erased; where applicable, the private master records an explicit inclusion justification.

## Freeze and confidentiality

The 72 immutable item IDs, final private Test master, question-only execution file, private scoring key, and SHA-256 records were frozen outside Git on 2026-08-28. The public repository contains no Test questions, gold answers, accepted-answer inventories, row-level reviewer rationales, adjudication records, model mappings, or private checksums.

This M3 result closes only the Test-content component of M4. It does not authorize model execution.
